It closes the month while nobody is watching.

A small trucking firm's July: 27 documents, one broker payment covering eight loads at once minus a factoring fee, a fuel card statement with fourteen fills, and one scan nobody can read. Press the button and leave. Archon posts the books, splits the remittance back across the loads it settles, finds what is missing, writes the letters that fix it, checks its own work and files the month.

27 documents are waiting.

What it did, step by step

Nine steps, no prompts, no approvals. This trail is written as the run happens and persisted with the books, so the owner can retrace a close they were not present for.

The month

One payment, split back across eight loads

This is the step that makes a haulier's month closeable, and it is not matching. The broker sent one credit covering several loads, minus a fee charged once on the batch. The books need each load settled at what it was actually paid, and the identity below has to close: what landed equals what the lines pay, less the fee.

What does not add up

Nine detectors, worst first by severity then by money. Every threshold is learned from this firm's own books: a charge is an outlier because it is far above what this firm normally pays that supplier, not because it crossed a figure someone wrote down.

What lands in the owner's inbox

A haulier does not open a bookkeeping console on the first of the month, because a haulier is driving. So the last thing the close does is write the owner a letter and put it where they already read their mail. This is the exact text that arrives. Nothing here is phrased by a model: every figure came out of the close above.

The letters it wrote to everyone else

Filed, not sent. This is the one human gate in the product. There are two outward edges and they have different rules. The letter above goes to the owner: their own books arriving at them, delivered without being asked for, because that is the point of doing the work overnight. The letters below go to brokers and suppliers, and they stop here. An email to a broker cannot be un-sent, so an unattended run does not get to send one. Every draft below is written, costed and filed; a person presses send.

The close checked its own work

Five gates run before Archon will call a month closed. A failure blocks the close and names the gate rather than filing a confident report nobody checked.

Per truck

Direct cost is fuel and maintenance, the two an owner can act on per truck. Insurance and driver pay stay at firm level: apportioning them would invent a figure.
