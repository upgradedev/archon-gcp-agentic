A small trucking firm's month: documents arrive in a bucket, one broker payment covers eight loads at once minus a factoring fee, a fuel card statement carries fourteen fills, and one scan nobody can read. Archon posts the books, splits the remittance back across the loads it settles, finds what is missing, writes the letters that fix it, checks its own work and files the month. Every tile below opens the ledger behind it.

Who owes whom

The register an owner actually opens, because it answers the two questions that decide whether payroll clears: who has not paid me, and who have I not paid. A load paid short leaves the difference open rather than counting as settled, which is the case a single receivables total hides. Age is measured to the end of the period being closed, never to today, so re-running an old month gives the same answer.

One payment, split back across the loads it settles

This is the step that makes a haulier's month closeable, and it is not matching. The broker sent one credit covering several loads, minus a fee charged once on the batch. The books need each load settled at what it was actually paid, and the identity below has to close: what landed equals what the lines pay, less the fee.

What does not add up

Nine detectors, worst first by severity then by money. Every threshold is learned from this firm's own books: a charge is an outlier because it is far above what this firm normally pays that supplier, not because it crossed a figure someone wrote down.

By kind

The owner's close package, composed and filed

A haulier does not open a bookkeeping console on the first of the month, because a haulier is driving. So the last thing the close does is write the owner a letter and hand it to a delivery seam. This is the exact letter, and the pill on it says what actually happened: delivered when a mail channel is configured, composed and filed when none is. Nothing here is phrased by a model: every figure came out of the close.

The letters it wrote to everyone else

Filed, not sent. This is the one human gate in the product. There are two outward edges and they have different rules. The letter above goes to the owner: their own books arriving at them, delivered without being asked for, because that is the point of doing the work overnight. The letters below go to brokers and suppliers, and they stop here. An email to a broker cannot be un-sent, so an unattended run does not get to send one. Every draft below is written, costed and filed; a person presses send.
